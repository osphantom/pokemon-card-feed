The sealed playbook

How to buy sealed English Pokémon cards at retail and sell them on eBay at a profit. All times are Eastern. Checked November 8, 2026.

How to read this. Retailers publish almost none of this, so claims carry a label.

  • Official comes from the retailer, eBay, or Pokémon.
  • Reported comes from news sites or restock trackers. They often disagree, and several sell alert services.
  • Estimate is calculated from past patterns.

Treat a Reported time as when to be ready, not as a promise. This is general information, not financial, tax, or legal advice.

Do stores restock at midday?

I couldn't find good evidence that any big-box store restocks cards at midday. The one solid midday pattern is online, at Pokémon Center. In stores, Walmart's card vendor is the closest thing: one guide says weekday mornings around 7 to 10 AM, while another says any time from Thursday through Saturday, with reps told to vary their day and time. A visit can land at midday, but nothing shows it usually does. Your own store's staff are the best source.

Get ready once

This setup works at every store, and it's most of what separates a real shopper from a slow one.

  • Keep one account per store, in your real name, with your current address and a valid card saved. Update expired cards now.
  • Set a default payment method on Walmart. Readers reported that a missing default caused a "don't have payment set up" error when entering drawings.
  • Bookmark the exact product page before a drop instead of searching for it during one.
  • Turn off your VPN and use a stable connection.
  • Write down the most you'll pay for each item, using the Flip List.
  • Keep every receipt. They support your taxes and any authenticity dispute.

Pokémon Center

This is the highest-value route. Exclusive Elite Trainer Boxes cost $59.99, and on November 8 the Delta Reign version was selling around $209 on TCGplayer.

The sign-up you've heard about: Early Access Official FAQ, as reported

Pokémon Center started this invitation program in April 2026. It's the closest thing to a sign-up that helps against bots.

  1. Subscribe to the Pokémon Center newsletter once. You must live in the US, Canada, or the UK.
  2. Use the same email as your Pokémon Center account so a later invite matches it.
  3. Search your inbox, promotions, and spam for "Early Access" around big launches.
  4. If you're picked, you'll get a unique, single-use, non-transferable link with an expiry date.
  5. Log in with the invited email and stay on one device and browser until you've checked out.

Pokémon Center doesn't publish its other selection criteria, and meeting the requirements doesn't guarantee an invite. Its FAQ says extra subscriptions or email addresses don't help, and that creating multiple accounts ends any invitations you've received.

When preorders open Reported

Preorders usually open early in the week, Monday through Wednesday. Pitch Black and Delta Reign both opened 37 days before street date, and Chaos Rising 39. The first two 30th Celebration waves opened 63 and 74 days ahead.

Next likely one: if Flowing Fists arrives on February 5, the same lead time points to December 28 to 30. Estimate Pokémon hasn't confirmed the set date, and holiday week could shift it.

Watchers can sometimes see it coming. On September 28, one newsletter reported Delta Reign stock loaded on the site, and the queue opened on September 30.

The queue Reported

  • All 10 documented US queue openings in 2026 fell between 10:09 AM and 1:13 PM. The median was 12:12 PM, and 5 of the 10 were on a Wednesday. That's a small sample.
  • Product appeared a median of 13 minutes after the queue opened, with a range of 5 to 77 minutes across 9 logged cases.
  • Weekends have been effectively dead for major drops.
  • A queue can open and close with no product going live, and joining one doesn't reserve anything.
  • The biggest launches take hours. 30th Celebration preorders reportedly ran 6 to 7 hours.

Once you're in line

  • Don't reload. Refreshing can cost your place, and fans reported being flagged as bots for refreshing too often.
  • Turn off your VPN. VPN users reported being flagged or dropped.
  • Use a wired connection and a browser with no extensions running, as one report advised.

Order risk

Recent reports describe waves of order cancellations aimed at bots and resellers, with some ordinary buyers caught too. Store terms that appeared in search results let Pokémon Center limit quantities per order, account, card, person, or household and reject orders. I couldn't open the current US terms, so read them yourself.

Delta Reign preorders also slipped after checkout went down for about six days. Pokémon Center's estimated-ship table now says mid-November. Don't list a preorder on eBay until it has actually shipped to you.

Walmart

Drawings are now the main route Reported

Entries ran from 5 to 6 PM on Oct 14, 21, 28, and Nov 4. Each drawing sets its own window, and the item list usually appears a day or two ahead, so confirm before you plan around it.

  1. Sign in at Walmart.com while the window is open.
  2. Open the item's drawing page. You can enter more than one drawing, and entry is free.
  3. Choose your shipping address, payment method, and quantity, then submit.
  4. Walmart picks entries at random after the window closes.

If you're picked, Walmart places the order and charges you automatically. You can wait up to 72 hours to find out. Results for the November 4 drawing were due by 6 PM on Saturday, November 7. Check your order history and email.

Check the price first. Drawing prices are often above MSRP. On November 4 the Delta Reign ETB was $60 against a $49.99 MSRP, which is close to break-even at today's market. The Ultra-Premium Collection was $224.97 against $179.99 and still clears a healthy profit. The dashboard runs these numbers for the latest drawing.

  • Only enter for a quantity you're happy to pay for.
  • Drawing items may not be returnable.
  • Orders under $35 may carry a delivery fee, and drawing orders can't be combined with your cart. A reader reported seeing Walmart+ free shipping on the entry screen.
  • I found nothing showing that Walmart+ improves your odds.

The Wednesday 9 PM queue Reported

This is the queue you've heard about. You join by loading a live product page, there's no way to save a spot early, and being in line or having an item in your cart doesn't reserve it. Sources now conflict on how much Pokémon goes through it. Most recent coverage says Pokémon moved to drawings in late August and the queue mostly carries other card brands, while one tracker logged 83% of 90 Walmart Pokémon restocks on Wednesdays between 9 PM and midnight.

Watch out for marketplace pricing

Walmart's Black Friday Pokémon pages mix in third-party sellers. One "deal" listed a Perfect Order Booster Bundle at $39.95, down from $50, when MSRP is $26.94. Look for "Sold and shipped by Walmart."

In stores Reported

Accounts conflict, as covered in the midday answer. Ask a toy-department employee which day your store's card vendor comes.

Terms

Walmart's terms of use, as shown in search results, define reselling as buying a product in order to sell it commercially to a third party, and they let Walmart cancel orders or restrict accounts. Walmart's page blocks automated readers, so read the current version yourself. A full-time plan should assume some orders will get canceled.

Target

New: Target drawings Reported

Target ran its first Pokémon drawing from November 2 to 4, for a $14.99 Destined Rivals 3-pack blister. Entries closed at 11 AM on Wednesday. There's only been one so far, so there's no schedule yet.

  • Sign in and enter from the product page. Entry is free.
  • Unlike Walmart, you aren't charged automatically. Winners are emailed and complete the purchase themselves.
  • Drawing orders ship only, with no pickup or same-day delivery.

Online drops Reported

Most trackers point to overnight on Fridays, though one puts the prime window at 3 to 6 PM and another says there's no confirmed schedule. Restockd logged about 85% of 314 restocks on Fridays between 3 and 6 AM. RestockR logged 220 restocks, with 50% on Fridays, 21% on Tuesdays, and 81% between 3 and 6 AM. Recent waves landed around 3 AM on Sep 11, Oct 16, and Nov 6.

Limits

  • Online: two per transaction and up to two transactions for the same product, according to Polygon's reporting.
  • For the 30th Celebration launch: reports of two per item, one transaction per card, and a "personal use only" policy.
  • On November 6, one store posted a limit of one per item, per guest. Enforcement varies by store.

Target's "notify me" alerts can help, but Target says they don't reserve stock, don't cover store inventory, and may exclude high-demand items.

Going in person

You mentioned camping out. I found no official Target policy on lining up, so ask your store first. Sources conflict on when shelves fill: one says Sunday and Monday mornings, another says Tuesday through Friday mornings. Floor staff usually know truck days but won't hold product. Ask politely:

  1. What's the current limit for each Pokémon item?
  2. Are cards on the shelf, behind the counter, or in a locked case?
  3. Do you allow lines before opening, and is there any policy for them?
  4. Which mornings do cards usually come in?

Your Target card

The Circle Card's 5% discount doesn't list trading cards among its exclusions, so it should apply. Confirm it on your receipt. Target says it doesn't accept resale exemption certificates, so you'll pay sales tax there even with a permit.

Sam's Club

  • A membership is required online and in the club. The limit is usually 2 per membership.
  • Reported Drops land on weekday mornings, about 7 to 11 AM Eastern, with Tuesday the most common day. One tracker says online drops hit at 11 PM instead.
  • Plus members occasionally get early access to hot drops.
  • Recent example: a Sam's-exclusive Focused Fighters Premium Collection dropped on Tuesday, July 21, at $54.98.

The trick worth knowing

Trackers say Sam's re-drops canceled orders and leftover stock in a smaller wave in the days after a main drop. Keep the item bookmarked for the following week.

In the club

Check front-of-store displays and the toy and seasonal aisles. Pallets often land overnight, and online and club stock move separately.

Costco

  • Treat it as a warehouse hunt. There's no confirmed drop time, and October through December has been the heaviest season.
  • Bundles can beat MSRP. Ascended Heroes ETBs were reported as low as $42.99 against $49.99 in some regions.
  • Limits are usually 1 or 2 per membership. Sources conflict on whether non-members can buy online, so count on needing a membership.
  • Set "My Warehouse" on Costco.com, check local availability, and call your warehouse with the item number before driving over.
  • Reported item number for the 30th Celebration ETB and Booster Bundle: #2007496, at about $57.49. Costco hasn't confirmed it.
  • When Costco runs an online drop, a tracker describes a virtual queue with random placement, so arriving early may not help.

Best Buy and Amazon

Neither is on your store list, but both have run invite-only drops. Best Buy sent invites for Prismatic Evolutions ETBs on October 30, and Amazon offered a 30th Celebration ETB at $89.99 by invite in September. Check your accounts for invitations, then compare the price with the Flip List. An invite doesn't mean it's a good price.

GameStop is left out, as you asked. Your read was right: reports put 30th Celebration ETBs at about $130 against a $50 MSRP.

Rules that follow from this

  • Chase Pokémon Center exclusives and special sets first.
  • Skip small items unless they're unusually hot. Shipping eats them.
  • Don't assume a booster box is the good buy. At MSRP most recent ones lost money on November 8.
  • A price above MSRP can still work, but only if the Flip List says so.
  • Confirm with eBay's sold listings, because market prices lag and asking prices aren't sales.

Prices move fast

Right now they're moving down in places. The 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collections were around $558 to $594 before release and about $384 to $416 two days after. One November report also describes reprint-driven drops in modern singles and corrections in sealed. Older hits have held up, with Prismatic Evolutions ETBs still reported at $180 to $220 a year on, though I found no reliable data on the best week to sell a new set. Anything you buy can lose value, including sealed product.

Selling on eBay

The fees Official

Your planFinal value fee on Collectible Card Games
No Store or Starter Store13.25% up to $7,500 per item, then 2.35%
Basic, Premium, Anchor, or Enterprise Store12.35% up to $2,500 per item, then 2.35%
Every plan, per order$0.30 on orders of $10 or less, $0.40 on orders over $10

The percentage applies to the item, shipping charged to the buyer, and the sales tax the buyer pays. At 7% tax, a 13.25% fee is really about 14.2% of your sale price. Sellers rated Below Standard pay an extra 6% on these fees.

Should you pay for a Store?

A Basic Store costs $21.95 a month billed yearly, or $27.95 month to month, and saves 0.90 percentage points. It pays for itself at about $2,440 a month in total sales on yearly billing, or about $3,105 on monthly. Premium has the same fee rate for cards, so it only helps if you need more listings. A Starter Store doesn't lower the rate at all.

Top Rated Plus Official

You get 10% off the final value fee, but not the per-order fee, on listings with same or one-day handling and 30-day free returns. You need at least 100 transactions and $1,000 in sales over 12 months. On an $86 ETB that's about $1.22 per sale. Weigh that against accepting 30-day free returns on sealed product, where a buyer could return a box that isn't the one you sent.

Selling before you have the item Official

eBay's presale policy requires "presale" in the title and description, the date the item will be available to ship, and a guarantee of shipping within 40 business days of purchase. Older guides saying 30 days are out of date. Violations can lead to ended listings and lower search placement.

Given how often retail orders get canceled or delayed, the safer habit is to list only what you're holding. A canceled sale also counts against you as a seller.

Listings that sell

  • Use a title like: Pokémon TCG Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box ETB Factory Sealed English.
  • Photograph the actual box, including the shrink wrap and seams.
  • Only use "New/Factory Sealed" for product that truly is. eBay defines it as brand-new, unopened, and undamaged.
  • Offer free shipping, and build it into the price. That's what the profit math assumes.
  • Check sold listings for that item before setting your price.

Packing and protecting yourself

  • Ship boxes in a box, not a mailer, with padding on every side.
  • For expensive items, photograph or film the seal and the packing.
  • Keep the retailer receipt for every unit.
  • Even with no returns, eBay's Money Back Guarantee covers "not as described" claims.
  • eBay Standard Envelope won't work for sealed boxes. It's limited to eligible cards under $20, up to 3 oz, with no boxes.
  • eBay's card-seller page says to add signature confirmation on items over $750, which can apply to cases and big lots.

What full-time takes

You told me full-time income is the goal, so here's the plain math. Say you want $4,000 a month in profit before income tax. That's about 410 Delta Reign ETBs a month at November 8's $9.79 each, or about 160 units at a $25 average. Stores limit you to one or two per item, and the items that earn $100 or more are the hardest to get.

Retail flipping alone is unlikely to get there, and it depends on stores that are actively working to limit resellers. It's a good way to build cash, feedback, and sales history. The step that changes the math is wholesale:

One third-party estimate puts a distributor ETB at about $35, with no sales tax when you buy on a resale certificate. At that cost, the same Delta Reign ETB earns about $28 instead of $10. That's what the shop section below is really about.

Taxes and paperwork

General information. A local accountant can tell you what applies to you.

  • Form 1099-K: for 2026, marketplaces generally send one when you pass both $20,000 in payments and 200 transactions. Some states have lower thresholds. Your income is taxable either way.
  • You'll want from the start: a separate bank account, a record of every purchase and sale, and all your receipts.
  • Full-time means self-employment. Expect self-employment tax and quarterly estimated payments. Inventory is generally deducted when it sells, not when you buy it.
  • Seller's permit or resale certificate: this lets you buy inventory without sales tax where it's accepted, and only for goods you'll resell. Cards you keep for your collection don't qualify. Keep those purchases separate.

The order to do things

  1. Register a business with your state, as an LLC or sole proprietorship.
  2. Get a free EIN from the IRS.
  3. Get your state's seller's permit or resale certificate. One guide says this takes 1 to 5 business days.
  4. Open a separate business bank account.
  5. Keep clean sales records. Distributors may ask for your history, and your eBay account helps here.
  6. Get insurance before holding much inventory. A homeowner's policy may exclude it, so ask about inland marine coverage.
  7. Apply to distributors well before you need product.

Distributors Reported

GTS, Southern Hobby, Alliance, and ACD appear on multiple lists of US distributors carrying Pokémon. Requirements below come from third-party guides, so confirm them with each distributor.

  • Common asks: an EIN letter, a resale certificate, and a business address.
  • Some want a photo of your storefront, and one guide says most require a physical location rather than a home address.
  • One guide cites 6 months or more in business, plus sales history.
  • First orders are reported at $250 to $1,000. Approval is reported at 3 to 10 business days.
  • Allocation goes by account history. One guide warns that if you open an account the week a hot set launches, you'll get little or none.
  • I couldn't confirm whether an eBay-only business qualifies. Online-friendly wholesalers exist at higher prices. Ask each distributor directly.

Play! Pokémon Store Official handbook, Sept 2026

  • Create a Pokémon Trainer Central account and Player ID, then submit a venue review application with accurate details. Photos and videos can't be AI-generated.
  • You'll agree to the Play! Pokémon Store Agreement. Some regions also require a background check.
  • Approved stores get the Retailer Portal and can run events.
  • Eligible stores can run Prereleases up to two weeks before street date, and the Early Release program lets them sell products from that expansion starting the Monday before the US release.

What to stay away from

You asked for every trick. These exist and I've left them out, because they break store rules and get orders canceled or accounts closed, which is a bad trade for a business that depends on those accounts.

  • Bots, auto-checkout tools, and queue-joining apps
  • Extra accounts, extra emails, or altered addresses to get around limits
  • Paying store employees to hold product, or paying "workers" to check out for you
  • "Guaranteed" checkout services
  • Listing products you don't have and can't be sure of getting
  • Resealed product, or anything described as factory sealed when it isn't
