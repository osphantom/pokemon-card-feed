# 🎯 How Target works

**Online drops** usually land overnight, about 3 to 6 AM Eastern, most often on Fridays. Set an alarm if the item is worth it.

**Drawings are new.** The first one ran November 2 to 4. It's free to enter from the product page. If you win, Target emails you and you finish the purchase yourself. Drawing orders ship only.

**Limits:** usually 2 online. One store posted 1 per item for the November 6 launch.

**Going to the store? Ask politely:**
1. What's the limit for each Pokémon item?
2. Are cards on the shelf, behind the counter, or locked up?
3. Do you allow lines before opening?
4. Which mornings do cards usually come in?

💳 Your Target Circle Card's 5% off should apply. Check your receipt.
