# 🛒 How Sam's Club works

**You need a membership** online and in the club. The limit is usually 2 per membership.

**When:** usually weekday mornings, about 7 to 11 AM Eastern, most often Tuesday. Sources don't fully agree.

**In the club:** check the front-of-store displays and the toy and seasonal aisles.

💡 **Worth knowing:** canceled orders and leftover stock can come back in a smaller wave a few days later. Keep the item bookmarked for a week.
