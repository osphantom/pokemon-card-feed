**From eBay seller to shop owner**
Full version: https://osphantom.github.io/pokemon-card-feed/playbook.html#shop

**Why wholesale matters:** at a reported $35 distributor cost, a Delta Reign ETB earns about $28 instead of $10.

**The order to do things**
1. Register a business (LLC or sole proprietorship).
2. Get a free EIN from the IRS.
3. Get your state's seller's permit or resale certificate.
4. Open a separate business bank account.
5. Keep clean sales records. Your eBay history helps.
6. Get insurance before holding much inventory.
7. Apply to distributors well before you need product. GTS, Southern Hobby, Alliance, and ACD appear on multiple lists.

**Cheapest to most involved**
eBay Store → card-show tables ($40–$550) → live selling → antique-mall case ($50–$150 a month) → vending machine ($2,850 and up) → small office storefront → retail shop ($25,000–$150,000 or more) → Play! Pokémon Store status.

Distributors allocate by account history, so an account opened the week a hot set launches gets little or none.
