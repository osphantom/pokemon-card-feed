# 🏷️ Best Buy stock

This is your own stock bot. It reads Best Buy's official stock data and pings you here when sealed Pokémon cards come back online or show up in a Maryland store.

**When you get a ping:** tap **Add to cart** right away, then compare the price with {{what-to-buy}}.

**In a store:** "in stock" comes from Best Buy's system, not a person checking the shelf. Reserve pickup online or call before you drive.

**Only you can see this channel.** Best Buy's rules limit using its data for other people, so this stays private even if friends join the server.

**Alerts delete themselves after 2 days.** Best Buy's rules limit how long its data can be kept. The list below always shows what's in stock now.
