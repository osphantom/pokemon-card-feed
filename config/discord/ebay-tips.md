# 🚚 Selling on eBay

**What eBay keeps on Pokémon cards**
• No Store or Starter Store: 13.25%
• Basic Store or higher: 12.35%
• Plus $0.40 per order ($0.30 on orders of $10 or less)
• The percentage also counts the buyer's sales tax, so plan on about 14%.

**Is a Basic Store worth it?** It costs $21.95 a month billed yearly. It pays for itself at about $2,440 a month in sales.

**What a product must sell for just to break even**
• ETB: about $75
• Pokémon Center ETB: about $87
• Booster bundle: about $43
• Booster box: about $217

**Selling before you have it (presale):** the listing must say "presale" in the title and description, give the ship date, and ship within 40 business days. It's safer to list only what you're holding.

**Protect yourself**
📸 Photograph the actual box and seal.
🧾 Keep every receipt.
📦 Ship boxes inside a box, with padding on every side.
