# 🏁 From eBay seller to shop owner

**Why it matters:** at a reported $35 wholesale cost, a Delta Reign ETB earns about $28 instead of $10.

**Do these in order**
1. Register a business (an LLC or sole proprietorship).
2. Get a free EIN from the IRS.
3. Get your state's seller's permit or resale certificate.
4. Open a separate business bank account.
5. Keep clean sales records. Your eBay history helps.
6. Get insurance before holding much inventory.
7. Apply to distributors well before you need product. GTS, Southern Hobby, Alliance, and ACD appear on several lists.

**Ways to sell, cheapest first**
eBay Store → card show tables ($40 to $550) → live selling → antique mall case ($50 to $150 a month) → vending machine ($2,850 and up) → small office storefront → retail shop ($25,000 to $150,000 or more) → official Play! Pokémon Store.

**Good to know:** distributors give stock based on account history, so an account opened the week of a hot release gets little or none.
