# 🔵 How Walmart works

**Drawings are the main way Pokémon sells now.** A drawing is a free lottery for the chance to buy.

**When:** lately Wednesdays, 5 to 6 PM Eastern. Each drawing sets its own time, so check {{next-drops}} and the posts below.

**How to enter**
1. Sign in at Walmart.com while the drawing is open.
2. Open the item's drawing page.
3. Pick your address, payment method, and quantity.
4. Submit. It's free, and you can enter more than one drawing.

**If you win, Walmart charges you automatically.** Results can take up to 72 hours. Check your email and order history.

⚠️ **Check the price first.** Drawing prices are often above the normal store price. {{what-to-buy}} shows what things sell for.

**Getting a payment error?** Set a default payment method and update your billing address.

**Online queue:** Wednesdays at 9 PM Eastern, but it now mostly carries other card brands.

**In stores:** a card vendor restocks on a day that varies by store. Ask someone in the toy department which day it comes.
