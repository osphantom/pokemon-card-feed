# 💰 Other flippable drops (beyond cards)

Cards aren't the only thing that flips. These run their **own** raffle/drop systems — not Walmart/Target/Best Buy — so you sign up on each one directly. Unlike the card boards, there's **no auto-priced profit here** (resale for sneakers, toys, and Lego can't be pulled reliably), so **check recent eBay *sold* prices yourself before you commit.**

**👟 Nike SNKRS (sneakers)** — the biggest flip market after cards.
• Make a Nike Member account, turn on push notifications, watch the **Upcoming** feed in the SNKRS app. Draws open for only **2–15 minutes**; winners are random. One entry per person.
• Flip the hyped ones (Jordan 1/4/11 retros, collabs). Check the exact colorway's sold prices first.

**🧱 LEGO Insiders (formerly VIP)** — exclusives sell out and resell high.
• Free LEGO Insiders account. **Insiders Weekend is Nov 21–22, 2026**; US in-store private shopping **Nov 15**. "Hard to Find" sets and retiring sets are the flips.
• Retiring sets (check lego.com "Retiring Soon") are the safest flip — buy at retail before they're gone.

**🧸 Pop Mart / Labubu (blind-box toys)** — still flips, but cooling.
• No public schedule; drops are teased on Pop Mart's app and socials. The app and popmart.com are **separate inventories** — watch both.
• Heads-up: Labubu demand fell sharply in late 2026 (Pop Mart reported ~45–50% lower quarterly revenue), so margins are thinner than the 2025 peak. Check sold prices — don't assume the old hype prices.

**🟣 Funko (Pop! exclusives)** — con/store exclusives and shared drops flip.
• Funko's own site drops + retailer exclusives (GameStop, Hot Topic). Grails and vaulted Pops hold value; common ones don't.

**🏈 Sports cards (Fanatics / Topps)** — allocated hobby boxes flip like Pokémon.
• Fanatics Collect drops and retailer allocations. Same game as Pokémon sealed: get it at retail, resell the hot sets.

**The honest priority:** your most reliable money is still **trading cards** (Pokémon + the other TCGs), because the invite/drawing systems are built to beat bots *and* we can price the resale automatically. Treat the list above as opportunistic — great when a hyped drop lands, but always sold-price-check first.
