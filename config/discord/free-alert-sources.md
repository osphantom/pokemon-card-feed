**Free real-time alerts**

This server checks about every 15 minutes and doesn't watch store pages itself. For alerts within seconds, add free sources that specialize in it. Pricing changes, so confirm they're still free.

**Free Discord servers**
• Restockd: https://restockd.app/blog/pokemon-discord
• TrackaLacker: https://www.trackalacker.com/articles/news/best-free-pokemon-restocks-discord-server
• PikaNotify (free alerts arrive 5 minutes late): https://pikanotify.com/
• Pokemon Restocks and Alerts: https://discord.com/servers/pokemon-restocks-and-alerts-1369077918244012072

**Pipe them into this server**
If one of those servers has an announcement channel, open it and press Follow, then choose #live-restocks here. Their alerts will show up in your own server.
How it works: https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028384531-Channel-Following-FAQ
