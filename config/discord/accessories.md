# 🧸 Flipping accessories

It's not just cards. Pokémon **accessories** flip too — exclusive plush, keyboards, mice and controllers, Funko Pops, Lego and Mega sets, puzzles, blankets, lunch boxes. If a store sells it and it goes for more on eBay, it's worth grabbing.

**How this works now**
• Your stock watcher checks every store for accessories as well as sealed cards.
• Each accessory is priced off a list of its **recent eBay selling prices** (the median of the last 30 days), refreshed twice a day — no guessing, no live lookups.
• You only get pinged when you'd keep about **$20 or more** after eBay fees, shipping, and sales tax — the same bar as cards.
• Brand-new exclusives join the list on their own as they start selling on eBay.

**Shipping is cheaper on small stuff**
• A pin, keychain, or small plush: about $4.
• A mug, mouse, or bigger plush: about $5.
• A backpack, controller, or Lego set: about $6–7.
• A keyboard, board game, or blanket: about $12.
Price your listing with that in mind — a small item doesn't cost $15 to ship.

**Before you buy to flip**
📈 Check the recent **sold** prices on eBay, not the asking prices.
🏷️ Make sure it's the store-exclusive version, not a common one that looks similar.
📦 Pad small items well — a cracked mug or squished box means a return.
