**Welcome to your Pokémon sealed feed.** This server fills itself about every 15 minutes. Sealed English Pokémon cards only: no clothing, plush, video games, or GameStop.

**Dashboard:** https://osphantom.github.io/pokemon-card-feed/
**Full playbook:** https://osphantom.github.io/pokemon-card-feed/playbook.html

**Where to look**
• #hot-alerts: preorders, queues, and drawings worth acting on
• #drop-reminders: heads-up before the weekly windows
• #today: morning summary at about 7 AM Eastern
• #flip-list: what clears a profit at retail price
• #live-restocks, #preorders, #deals, #market-moves, #release-calendar, #news: everything else, sorted

**Set your notifications once**
1. Right-click #hot-alerts, choose Notification Settings, then All Messages.
2. Do the same for #drop-reminders and #today.
3. Set the rest to Only @mentions so your phone stays quiet.

**Labels**
Official: stated by the retailer, eBay, or Pokémon.
Reported: seen by news sites or trackers, which often disagree.
Estimate: calculated from past patterns.
Listed: taken from a TCGplayer presale listing.

Times are Eastern. A Reported window means when to be ready, not a promise.
