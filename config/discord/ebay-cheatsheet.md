**eBay cheat sheet** (checked Nov 8, 2026)

**Fees on Collectible Card Games (Official)**
• No Store or Starter: 13.25% up to $7,500 per item
• Basic Store or higher: 12.35% up to $2,500 per item
• Per order: $0.30 at $10 or less, $0.40 above
• The percentage includes the buyer's sales tax, so 13.25% is about 14.2% of your price at 7% tax.

**Basic Store:** $21.95 a month billed yearly. Pays for itself at about $2,440 a month in total sales.

**Top Rated Plus:** 10% off the final value fee, but it requires one-day handling and 30-day free returns. On sealed product, weigh that against return-swap risk.

**Break-even sale price from retail**
• ETB: about $75 (1.50×)
• Pokémon Center ETB: about $87 (1.46×)
• Booster Bundle: about $43 (1.59×)
• Booster Box: about $217 (1.34×)

**Presale listings (Official)**
"Presale" must be in the title and description, the ship date must be listed, and you must ship within 40 business days. Safer habit: list only what you're holding.

**Protect yourself**
Photograph the actual seal. Keep every receipt. Ship boxes in boxes. Standard Envelope doesn't work for sealed product.
