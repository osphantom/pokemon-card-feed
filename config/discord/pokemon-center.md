# 🔴 How Pokémon Center works

**Why it matters:** exclusive Elite Trainer Boxes are usually the most profitable item you can buy at retail.

**When:** the queue usually opens on a weekday between 10 AM and 2 PM Eastern, most often on a Wednesday. Weekends have been quiet. There's no official schedule.

**How to get ready**
1. Sign up for the newsletter once. Invites to buy early go out by email.
2. Stay logged in, with your card and address saved.
3. Turn off any VPN.

**When the queue appears**
✅ Stay in line and keep the page open.
❌ Don't reload. You can lose your place or get flagged as a bot.
⏳ Product shows up about 13 minutes after the queue opens, but big launches can take hours.

**Preorders** usually open Monday to Wednesday, about 37 to 39 days before the street date.

**Know this:** extra accounts end your invitations, and Pokémon Center has canceled orders it thinks came from bots or resellers.
