**Store cheat sheet** (Eastern time, checked Nov 8, 2026)
Mostly Reported. Details and sources: https://osphantom.github.io/pokemon-card-feed/playbook.html

**Pokémon Center**
• Subscribe to the newsletter once for Early Access invites. Don't make extra accounts or emails. That ends invitations.
• Preorders usually open Mon–Wed, about 37 to 39 days before street date for main sets.
• Queue: weekdays 10 AM–2 PM, Wednesday busiest. Product appears about 13 minutes after it opens.
• In line: don't reload, no VPN, stable connection.

**Walmart**
• Drawings: Wed 5–6 PM lately. Free to enter at Walmart.com. Each sets its own window.
• If you're picked, you're charged automatically. Check the price first, because it's often above MSRP.
• Queue: Wed 9 PM, now mostly other card brands.
• In store: ask the toy department which day the card vendor comes.

**Target**
• New drawings began Nov 2. Winners are emailed and buy manually. Ships only.
• Online: Fridays, about 3–6 AM.
• Limits: 2 online; one store posted 1 per item for Nov 6.
• Ask your store about lines, locked cases, and which mornings cards come in.
• No resale certificates accepted.

**Sam's Club**
• Weekday mornings, most often Tuesday, about 7–11 AM. Limit usually 2 per membership.
• Canceled orders get re-dropped days later, so keep items bookmarked for a week.
• Check front-of-store displays and toy and seasonal aisles.

**Costco**
• Warehouse hunt with no schedule. Busiest October through December.
• Set My Warehouse, then call with the item number before driving over.
