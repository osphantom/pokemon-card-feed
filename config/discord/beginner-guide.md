# 🧭 Your first drop, in 5 steps

**1. Set up your store accounts.** Use one account per store, with your real name, address, and card saved. Extra accounts can get your orders canceled.

**2. Join the Pokémon Center newsletter.** That's how invites to buy early are sent. Sign up once, with the same email as your Pokémon Center account.

**3. Watch {{next-drops}}.** It shows the next drop at each store, with a live countdown.

**4. Check {{what-to-buy}} before spending money.** Many products lose money after eBay fees.

**5. Keep every receipt.** You'll need them for taxes and if a buyer ever files a complaint.

# 📖 Words you'll see

**MSRP:** the normal store price.
**Drop:** when a store puts new stock up for sale.
**Restock:** a product that sold out comes back.
**Street date:** the day a product officially goes on sale.
**Preorder:** paying now for a product that ships later.
**Queue:** an online waiting line. Don't refresh the page while you're in one.
**Drawing or lottery:** a free random pick for the chance to buy.
**ETB (Elite Trainer Box):** a box with 9 packs and accessories, usually $49.99.
**Pokémon Center ETB:** an exclusive version, usually $59.99. These tend to be the most profitable.
**Booster bundle:** 6 packs, usually $26.94.
**Booster box:** 36 packs, usually $161.64.
**UPC (Ultra-Premium Collection):** a large premium box.
**Flip:** buying something to resell it.

# 🏷️ What the labels mean

**Official:** stated by the store, eBay, or Pokémon.
**Reported:** seen by news sites or trackers. These are usual times, not promises.
**Estimate:** calculated from past patterns.
**Listed:** taken from a TCGplayer presale listing.
